   提示词：`A Three Kingdoms era wooden battering ram siege cart, sloped plank roof covered with hides, a heavy iron-capped log hanging on ropes under the roof, four solid wooden wheels, seen from the front-top three-quarter view, facing away toward the top of the image, bright painted game illustration, plain mid-gray background, single object, centered with margin, no text`
2. **友军将旗**：竖杆青色方旗，旗上空白（姓氏游戏里写）；再画一张倒在地上的。
3. **井**：石砌井栏 + 木辘轳；再画一张发金光（藏玉玺）的。
4. **赤帻**：插在木杆上的一块红头巾，随风飘（战场用），外加一张城头小图标。
