# 10-29 深夜：法术纹理（照用户发的黑洞特效参考，「这个级别」）

用途：主体用画好的半透明纹理在游戏里旋转、流动、溶解，不再用代码画线。
**全部画在纯黑底上**（游戏里用「加亮」叠加，黑色等于透明），不要文字、不要边框，四周留黑。

1. **星云旋涡盘**（黄天当立主体，正俯视、圆形，游戏里压扁成斜躺）
```
Top-down view of a swirling nebula accretion disk, spiral arms of wispy blue-violet glowing gas and fine stardust curling inward, a bright broken white ring around an empty pitch-black center, soft feathered edges fading to black, painted on a pure black background for additive blending, circular composition centered, no text
```
2. **能量飘带**（绕旋涡飞转，横条）
```
A single long flowing wisp of luminous blue-white energy ribbon, curving gently, soft feathered edges with a thin bright white core, tapering to fine points at both ends, a few faint sparkles along it, painted on a pure black background, horizontal composition, no text
```
3. **光瓣绽放**（「爆」那一下）
```
A blooming flower made of large translucent glowing petals of blue-violet light, petals radiating outward from a bright white center, soft wispy edges like silk or smoke, a few thin light rays shooting out between petals, painted on a pure black background, centered, no text
```
4. **星芒**（六个一张，游戏里切开用）
```
Six separate small star sparkle flares on a pure black background arranged in a 3x2 grid with space between them: four-point and eight-point cross glints with soft round halos, white with a faint blue tint, varying sizes, no text
```
5. **火焰条**（华雄地火，横向可无缝拼接）
```
A horizontal strip of rising hand-painted flame tongues, wispy and uneven heights, deep crimson base, orange body, pale yellow-white tips, a few embers floating above, painted on a pure black background, seamless left and right edges, wide composition 4:1, no text
```
6. **火龙身**（吕布火龙，横条，游戏里沿身子弯曲）
```
A long horizontal strip texture of a Chinese fire dragon's body made of swirling flames and glowing ember scales, serpentine flowing fire, orange-red with a white-hot core line along the spine, feathered fiery edges, painted on a pure black background, seamless along its length, very wide composition 6:1, no text
```
7. **火龙头**（单张）
```
The head of a Chinese fire dragon made entirely of flames, side view facing right, open jaws, long flowing whiskers and horns of fire, orange-red with white-hot glowing eyes and core, painted on a pure black background, centered, no text
```
